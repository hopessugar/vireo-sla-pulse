Data Processing Pipeline

  • Loaded -- raw rows from tickets.csv
  • Removed -- duplicate tickets (migration re-imports per Sameer)
  • Excluded open/pending tickets from breach calculation
  • Normalized CSAT: legacy 0 treated as null (no response, not a rating)
  • All timestamps converted from UTC to IST (UTC+5:30) for shift assignment

SLA Thresholds (support-policy.pdf Section 3)

  • Chat: 15 minutes first response
  • Voice callback: 2 hours (120 min)
  • Social: 4 hours (240 min)
  • Email: 8 hours (480 min)

Each breach triggers Rs 350 store credit (Section 3). Attributed to the resolving agent.

Shift Definitions (IST, Section 7)

  • Morning: 06:00 - 14:00 IST
  • Day: 14:00 - 22:00 IST
  • Night: 22:00 - 06:00 IST

Agent Assignment (Temporal Join)

Roster uses from_date / to_date ranges. Agent's shift at ticket creation time is used, correctly handling the June 2025 Indore reshuffle (A3002, A3003 moved Night to Day).

AI Classification Validation

  • Rule-based classifier validates bot categories against customer message text

Known Limitations

  • ~40 failed IVR transcripts included (valid timestamps, just garbled text)
  • Tier 2 agents (Escalations & Warranty) measured on resolution days per Section 6 -- breach rate comparison with Tier 1 is not apples-to-apples
  • Weekly trend for partial weeks (first/last) may show skewed rates
  • Rule-based classifier has lower accuracy on "Other" (catch-all category)
